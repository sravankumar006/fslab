About Our College

Welcome to ANITS Engineering College. Our college provides quality education in engineering and technology.

ANITS (Anil Neerukonda Institute of Technology & Sciences) is one of the better-known private engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh. It was established in 2001, is autonomous, permanently affiliated with Andhra University, and several engineering programs are NBA accredited. The institute has also appeared in the NIRF engineering rankings2025 and has a strong emphasis on placements and industry connections. from ture sources.
